About SonicBeast

SonicBeast is an advanced mobile audio engine featuring multi-band compression, dynamic headroom limiters, and acoustic studio tuning for Android devices.

  • Up to +15 dB Gain: Clean amplification powered by Android DynamicsProcessing engine.
  • Acoustic Tuning Suite: Cinema 3D, Audiophile Studio, Voice Clarity, and Beast Bass modes.
  • Device Routing: Independent sound profiles for Bluetooth A2DP, USB DACs, wired headsets, and built-in speakers.
  • Zero Data Tracking: 100% on-device processing. No accounts, no data collection.
