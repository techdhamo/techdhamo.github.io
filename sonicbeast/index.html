What it does

Raise and shape the sound on your phone

One slider for volume, five bands for tone, and modes for what you are listening to.

Volume boost

Raise the output level with a single slider. A limiter guards against harsh clipping, and the maximum boost is capped.

5-band equalizer

Adjust five frequency bands, pick a preset, or save your own settings.

Sound modes

Switch between Direct Boost, Cinema, Audiophile Studio, Voice Clarity, Beast Bass and Voice Focus to suit what you are playing.

Device profiles Pro

When headphones or a Bluetooth device connect, SonicBeast applies the settings you saved for that output.

Safety and privacy

Loud is a choice. So is your data.

Hearing notice

Loud audio can harm your hearing and your speaker. Start low and increase slowly, especially with headphones.

  • A 12 dB ceiling

    The boost never goes above a fixed 12 dB ceiling, and each plan has its own lower limit. A limiter guards against harsh clipping.

  • Nothing is sent until you accept

    On first launch the app asks whether you accept analytics and crash reports. If you decline, the app works the same way.

  • Only two Firebase services, only after consent

    If you accept, SonicBeast uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. You can withdraw at any time in settings.

  • No account, no recording

    There is nothing to sign up for. The app does not use the microphone, and audio is never recorded or uploaded.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does it work during calls?

No. SonicBeast works on the sound of other apps, such as music players, podcasts and video apps. It does not boost call audio.

Does it record anything?

No. SonicBeast does not use the microphone and does not record, store or upload the audio you play.

Does it keep running in the background?

Yes. A foreground service keeps the effects active while you use other apps. With Pro, it keeps running after you swipe the app away and, where Android allows, restores your saved level after a reboot or when you next open the app.

Why is there a notification?

Android requires an ongoing notification while a foreground service is running. SonicBeast uses it to show that the volume engine is active and to give you volume controls.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open Google Play and go to Payments and subscriptions to manage or cancel. A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
