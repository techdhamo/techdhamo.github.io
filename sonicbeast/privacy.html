Privacy Policy for SonicBeast

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SonicBeast ("we", "us", or "our"), developed by Dhamo, handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

SonicBeast is built with privacy-by-design. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal identifiable information (PII). The app operates locally on your device.

2. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

The following non-identifiable preference data is stored solely in local Android DataStore on your device:

  • Volume boost preferences, master percentage, and limiter settings
  • Acoustic tuning mode selections and custom equalizer band adjustments
  • Audio routing preferences (e.g., Bluetooth, wired headset, speaker profiles)
  • Safety and extreme volume liability acknowledgement flags

None of this preference data ever leaves your device.

3. Permissions Used by SonicBeast

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Required to show an active foreground service notification enabling you to control volume levels and acoustic modes directly from your lock screen and notification shade.
  • BLUETOOTH_CONNECT (Android 12+): Required solely to detect when external Bluetooth headphones or speakers connect or disconnect, allowing SonicBeast to display the correct active device sink. No Bluetooth identifiers or location data are stored or transmitted.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Required to apply system audio effects (DynamicsProcessing, Equalizer, BassBoost, Virtualizer).
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required to keep audio enhancement active while other media players (YouTube, Spotify, etc.) are playing in the background.

4. Third-Party Services & Payments

SonicBeast uses official Google Play services for maintenance and transactions:

  • Google Play In-App Billing: All subscriptions and Pro passes are processed directly and securely by Google Play. We never see, collect, or store your credit card or financial information.
  • Google Play In-App Updates & Reviews: Standard Google Play Core APIs to notify you of available app updates and offer store ratings.

5. Children's Privacy

SonicBeast is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

6. Health & Hearing Protection Disclaimer

Excessive volume levels can cause permanent hearing damage or hardware stress to mobile speakers. SonicBeast provides built-in safety limiters and warnings. By using this application, users assume responsibility for safe listening volume levels.
