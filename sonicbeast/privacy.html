SonicBeast Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what the SonicBeast app ("we", "us"), published by Dhamodaran Narayana Perumal (Dhamo), handles on your device and what leaves it. SonicBeast raises and shapes the volume of audio playing on your phone. You can reach us at support@dhamo.in.

1. What stays on your device

  • Audio is processed on your device by the Android audio effects framework and the app's own engine. SonicBeast does not use the microphone and does not record, store or upload the audio you play.
  • Your settings (boost level, equalizer bands, device profiles, safety acknowledgements, consent choice) are stored on your device only.

2. What we collect, and only after you accept

On first launch SonicBeast asks for your consent to analytics and crash reporting. Until you accept, both stay switched off and nothing is sent. If you decline, the app works the same way.

If you accept, we use Google's Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics: an app-instance identifier, app version, Android version, device model, approximate region (Google derives it from the IP address), and events that show which features are used (for example, a boost level was changed). Events carry no free text and no audio.
  • Crashlytics: crash reports and diagnostics with a stack trace, device model, Android version, app version and the app state at the time of the crash, so that we can fix crashes.
  • Install referrer: Firebase Analytics may read the Google Play install referrer (which campaign or store page led to the install) to measure installs. This also happens only after you accept.

We use this data to find and fix problems and to decide which features to improve. We do not sell it and do not use it for advertising. SonicBeast shows no ads and does not use the advertising ID: the app removes the AD_ID permission.

Data is processed by Google under Google's terms and privacy policy for Firebase (see firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

3. Accepting and withdrawing consent

You can change your choice at any time in the app settings. When you withdraw consent, collection stops at once, the app asks Firebase to reset its analytics data for this installation, and unsent crash reports on the device are deleted. To ask about data already sent, write to support@dhamo.in.

4. Purchases

SonicBeast Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and shows the app a purchase token and entitlement status so the app can unlock Pro. We never see your card or payment details. This is governed by Google Play's terms and privacy policy.

5. Permissions

SonicBeast declares the following permissions, including those added by the libraries it uses. Each is used only for the purpose given.

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: keeps the volume engine running as a foreground service while you use other apps.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: the foreground service type for this service. SonicBeast applies audio effects to the sound of other apps and plays no media of its own.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: shows the ongoing notification with volume controls (Android 13 and later).
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: lets the volume engine start again after the phone restarts, if you left it on.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: needed to attach the equalizer and dynamics effects to the audio output.
  • BLUETOOTH_CONNECT: detects which Bluetooth headphones or speaker are connected so the right device profile is applied. Bluetooth names and addresses are not sent anywhere (Android 12 and later).
  • INTERNET: lets Firebase send analytics and crash reports after you accept, and lets Google Play Billing reach Google Play.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: lets Firebase and Google Play Billing check whether a connection is available before sending.
  • WAKE_LOCK: lets Firebase finish sending a report while the screen is off; the app itself also holds a wake lock only while the volume engine is active.
  • BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE: the Google Play install referrer permission (com.google.android.finsky.permission.BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE), used as described in section 2 after you accept.
  • BILLING: the Google Play Billing permission (com.android.vending.BILLING), used for the Pro purchase in section 4.
  • DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION: an app-own permission (in.dhamo.sonicbeast.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION) added by AndroidX so that other apps cannot send broadcasts to the app's internal receivers. It grants nothing to other apps.

6. Children

SonicBeast is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly gather information from children.

7. Hearing safety

Loud audio can damage hearing and speakers. SonicBeast limits the maximum boost and shows a warning, but you remain responsible for a safe listening level.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes and, if the change affects what is collected, the app tells you. Contact: support@dhamo.in
