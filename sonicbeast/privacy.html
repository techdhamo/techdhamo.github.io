SonicBeast Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 11, 2026 • OFFICIAL COMPLIANCE DOCUMENT

Zero Personal Data Collection Policy

SonicBeast is built with privacy-by-design. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal identifiable information (PII). All audio processing operates 100% locally on your device.

01. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

The following non-identifiable preference data is stored solely in local Android DataStore on your device:

  • Volume boost levels, limiter thresholds, and safety acknowledgements
  • Acoustic tuning mode selections and custom 5-band equalizer adjustments
  • Audio routing preferences (e.g. Bluetooth A2DP, wired headset, built-in speaker)
  • SonicBeast Pro pass activation timestamps and tokens

None of this preference data ever leaves your device or touches any remote server.

02. Permissions Requested & Their Purpose

POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)

Required solely to display the active foreground service notification, allowing you to monitor amplification and adjust modes directly from your lock screen and notification shade.

BLUETOOTH_CONNECT (Android 12+)

Required solely to detect when external Bluetooth headphones or speakers connect or disconnect, enabling SonicBeast to switch to your saved Bluetooth sound profile automatically. No Bluetooth identifiers or location data are stored or transmitted.

MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK

Required to apply Android audio effect chains (DynamicsProcessing, Equalizer, Virtualizer) and ensure continuous volume amplification while other media apps (YouTube, Spotify) play in the background.

03. Third-Party Services & Payment Processing

All subscriptions and Pro passes are processed directly and securely through Google Play In-App Billing. We never see, handle, or store your credit card, bank details, or payment credentials. Standard Google Play transaction receipts are verified locally.

04. Children's Privacy

SonicBeast is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit any personal information from children.

05. Acoustic Health & Hearing Protection

Excessive audio exposure can cause irreversible acoustic damage. SonicBeast incorporates built-in hardware limiters and safety confirmation dialogs to encourage responsible volume levels.
