RemoteBeast

A remote for Google TV and Android TV on your phone. It talks to your TV directly over your home Wi-Fi, with a D-pad, touchpad, keyboard and volume that moves exactly as far as you press.

Coming soon to Google Play See what it does

Requires Android 7.0 or later, and a Google TV or Android TV on the same Wi-Fi network as your phone.

What it does

Your TV remote, on your phone

Pair once with the code on your TV screen, then control it from the couch.

D-pad and touchpad

Move through menus with the D-pad or swipe on the touchpad. Home, Back, media keys and power are one tap away.

Precise volume

One tap is one step. Or drag the volume bar to the level you want, and RemoteBeast takes the TV there and stops.

Keyboard

Type searches and passwords on your phone's keyboard instead of picking letters one by one on the TV.

Voice search Pro

Hold the voice button and speak. Your voice goes straight to your TV's own voice search.

Privacy

Your TV, your network, your choice

  • Straight to your TV

    Button presses, typing and volume go directly from your phone to your TV over your home Wi-Fi, encrypted. There is no RemoteBeast server in between.

  • Nothing is sent until you accept

    On first launch the app asks whether you accept analytics and crash reports. If you decline, the app works the same way. You can change your mind in Settings.

  • Your voice goes only to your TV

    Voice search streams the microphone to your paired TV while you hold the button. RemoteBeast does not record or keep it.

  • No account needed

    There is nothing to sign up for. Your paired TVs are saved on your phone only. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; Pro removes them.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which TVs does it work with?

TVs and streaming boxes running Google TV or Android TV that accept the standard Android TV remote connection. Your phone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

How do I pair my TV?

Open RemoteBeast on the same Wi-Fi as your TV and pick the TV from the list. The TV shows a short code; type it into the app. You only do this once per TV.

Can it turn my TV on?

With Pro, Wake TV switches the TV on when the TV stays connected to the network in standby. Some TVs turn their Wi-Fi off in deeper standby after a while; those can only be turned on with their own remote. On many TVs, a setting such as "Network standby" or an energy mode that keeps network features on helps.

Does it work away from home?

No. RemoteBeast talks to your TV over your home Wi-Fi, so your phone needs to be on the same network as the TV.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open Google Play and go to Payments and subscriptions to manage or cancel. A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
