Laddu Paiyan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what Laddu Paiyan ("we", "us"), a live stage for YouTube published by Dhamo Labs and built on our GlitterStage engine, handles and where it goes. It covers the web app at laddupaiyanlive.web.app and the desktop app for Mac and Windows. You can reach us at support@dhamo.in.

1. Google sign-in and YouTube

To go live, you sign in with your Google account and allow Laddu Paiyan to manage your YouTube account (the YouTube permission "youtube.force-ssl"). The app uses YouTube API Services only to run your stream:

  • create a live broadcast and a live stream on your channel, connect them, start and end the broadcast, with the title and privacy setting you choose;
  • read the live chat of your broadcast, so that messages, quiz answers and raffle entries can appear on your stage;
  • post messages in your live chat as your channel, only when you send a reply or start a quiz from the app.

Information from YouTube is used only for these purposes, on your device. We do not keep it on our own servers (we have none for this), we do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising. Laddu Paiyan's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

By using the YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove Laddu Paiyan's access to your Google account at any time on the Google account permissions page.

2. Sign-in tokens

  • Web app: the access token from Google is kept in memory only while the page is open.
  • Desktop app: the access token is kept in memory; the refresh token is stored encrypted on your computer with the operating system's secure storage, so you stay signed in. Signing out in the app deletes it. To also withdraw access on Google's side, use the permissions page linked above.

3. Your viewers' chat

Live chat messages contain your viewers' YouTube display names and what they write. Laddu Paiyan reads them only from your own broadcast, shows them on your stage when you choose, and uses them for quizzes, raffles and the leaderboard. Quiz, raffle and leaderboard results (display names and scores) are saved in the app's storage on your device so they survive a restart. They are not sent to us. They stay until you clear the app's or browser's site data. Anything shown on your stage is broadcast to your audience on YouTube.

4. Camera and microphone

The camera and microphone are used only after you switch them on in the app and your browser or operating system has asked for permission. The picture and sound go into your stage and, while you are live, into your stream on YouTube. Laddu Paiyan does not record them or send them anywhere else.

5. Settings stored on your device

Your look, theme, fonts, scenes, stickers, sounds, rules and other settings are stored in the app's storage on your device (browser local storage on the web). Music files you add are played from memory for the current session only; they are not uploaded or saved, and the music becomes part of your stream's sound while you are live. The desktop app also keeps, on your computer only, a log file of app steps, timings and error messages (with the stream key hidden, and without chat text or sign-in tokens) and crash dumps for troubleshooting. Neither is uploaded.

6. Usage analytics and error reports

Laddu Paiyan uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand which features are used and to catch errors. It is switched on when you use the app. What is sent:

  • events for actions and settings, for example a theme or sound was chosen, a quiz started, a broadcast was created or went live, or a step failed (as a short code);
  • counts and lengths instead of content: how many chat messages arrived, how long a stage text is;
  • a pseudonymous identifier, browser or app version, device type and approximate region, which Google derives from the IP address. On the web, Google Analytics stores its identifier in your browser (a cookie or local storage).

Never sent: chat messages, viewers' names, your replies, quiz answers, email addresses, passwords, sign-in tokens or stream keys. We use this data only to fix problems and improve the app. We do not sell it. It is processed by Google under its terms and privacy policy for Firebase (see firebase.google.com/support/privacy). You can block it with your browser's privacy or tracking-protection settings, or ask us to delete data by writing to support@dhamo.in.

7. Children

Laddu Paiyan is a tool for channel owners and is not directed at children under 13. The "made for kids" setting of each broadcast is yours to choose, as YouTube requires.

8. Security and retention

Connections to Google and YouTube use HTTPS. Data on your device stays until you sign out, clear the app's or browser's data, or uninstall the app. Data held by Google is kept according to Google's settings and policies.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes and, if the change affects what is collected, the app tells you. Contact: support@dhamo.in
