Laddu Paiyan

A live stage for YouTube, built on our GlitterStage engine. Put text, effects, quizzes, raffles and your live chat on screen, and go live on your channel.

Private preview See what it does

Early version. A web app, and desktop apps for Mac and Windows to go live. Free, with no ads.

What it does

A stage for your live show

Build the picture, bring your audience into it, and stream it to YouTube.

Stage

Big text with themes, fonts, looks and motion, particle effects, backgrounds, music and sound effects, plus your camera and microphone.

Live chat on stage

Show your YouTube live chat on screen and reply to it from the control panel.

Quizzes and raffles

Run timed quizzes and raffles from your chat, reveal the answer on stage, and keep a leaderboard.

Go live on YouTube

Sign in with Google, set a title and privacy, and stream the stage to your channel from the desktop app.

Privacy

Your channel, your viewers, your device

  • YouTube access only to run your stream

    The app uses YouTube to create and run your broadcast, read your live chat and post the replies you send. You can remove its access in your Google account at any time.

  • Chat stays on your device

    Viewers' names and messages are read from your own broadcast and kept on your device for quizzes, raffles and the leaderboard. They are not sent to us.

  • Analytics without content

    Usage analytics record actions and settings, never chat text, names, replies, tokens or stream keys.

  • Contests are yours to run fairly

    If you run a quiz or raffle, you set the rules and award the prizes, following YouTube's contest policies. Never charge viewers to take part.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is it for?

YouTube creators who host interactive live shows: quizzes, raffles and on-screen chat, with a stage that looks like a show rather than a webcam.

What do I need to go live?

A YouTube channel that can live stream, and the desktop app on a Mac or Windows computer with a steady upload connection.

Does it cost anything?

No. The app is free, shows no ads and has no purchases.

Can I get it now?

It is in private preview while we finish the desktop builds. Write to support@dhamo.in if you would like to try it.
